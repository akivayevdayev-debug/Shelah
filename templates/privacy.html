{{ _('privacy.5_international_data_transfers') }}

Several of our sub-processors process data in the United States. If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US or other countries outside your jurisdiction. As confirmed 2026-09-04 from each provider's own public DPF/DPA documentation: Clerk and Vercel are self-certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), including its UK and Swiss extensions, with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated as a fallback where DPF does not apply; Google participates in the DPF and additionally relies on SCCs for transfers outside DPF's coverage; Anthropic documents SCCs (EU Module 2/3, plus UK and Swiss transfer addenda) as its primary transfer mechanism and separately participates in the DPF as a secondary basis. As confirmed 2026-09-05 from their own current public documentation, Supabase relies solely on SCCs (Module 2/3, plus UK and Swiss transfer addenda), with no DPF self-certification found in its DPA; Sentry is self-certified under the EU-U.S. DPF, with SCCs (Module 2/3, plus a UK addendum) incorporated as a fallback where DPF does not apply. See docs/PRIVACY_OPERATIONS.md §3 for source links. These are the standard transfer safeguards recognized by the European Commission (Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework), and most services that rely on US-based providers use them for this purpose. Sh'elah relies on each vendor's own executed safeguard as confirmed above, and will review this section if a vendor's certification or DPA terms materially change.

{{ _('privacy.9_ai_processing_automated_decision_making') }}

Your question, the earlier turns of the same conversation and the source excerpts found for it are sent to Google Gemini, or to Anthropic Claude if Gemini is unavailable, to write the answer. If you are signed in and short summaries of your earlier questions are stored for your account, up to two of them are sent too. This is automated, but it does not produce a legal or similarly significant decision about you. It writes study material, and the answer tells you to ask your own rabbi. See our {{ _('privacy.ai_disclosure') }} page for how this works and where it goes wrong.
