{{ _('licenses.extended_to_shelahs_own_ai_output') }} this obligation is not limited to a user's own downstream reuse. It also covers Sh'elah's own act of serving an AI Output. Where an AI Output substantially synthesizes retrieved Wikipedia content (the last-resort case described in the AI Disclosure page), that specific answer is treated as CC-BY-SA Adapted Material of the underlying Wikipedia passage: it is attributed to Wikipedia and made available under CC-BY-SA 4.0 for that specific answer. This overrides the general "personal use only" Content license in the Terms of Service §9, which does not apply to this subset of AI Output. This is a deliberately conservative operator choice, adopted 2026-09-06 without a definitive legal determination of whether such synthesis actually creates an "Adapted Material" obligation under CC BY-SA 4.0 §1.1. The choice was made because treating the obligation as applicable carries less risk than assuming it does not.
